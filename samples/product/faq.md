# Acme Router X2 FAQ

**What does error E-4471 mean?**
The firmware update failed. Unplug the router for 30 seconds, then retry the update from the admin page.

**What is the warranty?**
The X2 has a 2-year limited warranty. It can be extended to 4 years by registering the device within 60 days of purchase.

**Default admin address**
Open http://192.168.8.1 and sign in with the password printed on the label under the device.
