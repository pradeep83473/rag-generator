# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 26 weeks of paid leave. Non-birth parents receive 6 weeks of paid leave, which can be taken any time in the first year.
